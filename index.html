Privacy Policy for 2048

Effective date: 11.08.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ABInteractive Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the 2048 mobile game ("the App").

1. Information stored by the App

The App does not require you to create an account. Your game score and best score may be stored locally on your device using the device's app storage so that your progress can be retained between sessions. We do not operate a game account service or a game-profile server for this App.

2. Advertising and third-party services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive information from the App and your device, such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, ad interaction information, diagnostics, and other data, as described in Google's applicable policies and disclosures. This information may be used for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation.

Advertising data practices, personalization, and consent choices may depend on your region, device settings, and the consent options presented to you. Where required, we use Google's consent tools to request and manage your advertising privacy choices.

For more information, review Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. How we use information

Locally stored game information is used to provide the game's score and best-score features. Information processed by the advertising service is handled for the purposes described by Google, including delivering and measuring ads and maintaining the security and operation of its advertising services.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your locally stored game scores. Information collected or processed by the AdMob SDK may be shared with Google and its service providers or advertising partners as described in Google's policies and the consent choices applicable to you.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your game score and best score remain in the App's local storage until you clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or the data is otherwise removed by your device. We do not maintain a game account or a game-score database on our servers. Data handled by Google through its advertising services is retained according to Google's policies.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not designed specifically for children under the age applicable in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising and audience settings must be configured in accordance with the App's actual target audience and applicable laws.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the App and its configuration appropriate for its intended use. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party services are subject to their own security practices and policies.

8. Your choices

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. You may also be able to manage advertising privacy and personalization choices through the consent options presented in the App and your device settings, where available.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this same URL, with its effective date updated.
